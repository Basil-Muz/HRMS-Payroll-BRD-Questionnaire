# Day Off Management (Employee Portal)

## Off Day Management

The Off Day Management module in the employee portal allows employees to swap their designated off days with prior approval, ensuring flexibility while maintaining operational balance.

**Key Features:**

Off Day Swap Request: Employees can raise requests to swap their weekly off Saturdays with another working Saturday, providing reasons and preferred dates.

Managerial Approval Workflow: Swap requests are routed to reporting managers for review and approval, ensuring team coverage is not compromised.

Real-Time Update: Once approved, the updated off day is reflected in the employee’s attendance schedule.

Audit Trail: Every swap request and approval is logged for future reference and compliance tracking.

## Off Day Swap Request

Employees can raise requests to swap their weekly off Saturdays with another working Saturday, providing reasons and preferred dates. Both the designated off Saturdays are listed in the request screen. Request can be made to swap one or both Saturdays.

## Off Day Swap Request Approval

Off day swap requests by direct reports are approved by immediate supervisor. This requires single level approval.

## Off Day Swap Sanctioning Cancellation

Off day swap requests that are approved by the immediate supervisor can be canceled through this option. This is required if the employee changes mind or swap approval has to be canceled due to work requirements.

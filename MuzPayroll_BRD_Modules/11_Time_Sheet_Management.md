# Time Sheet Management (Employee Portal)

## Time Log Management

Time Log Management is a crucial feature in the employee portal that helps track and manage employees' work hours efficiently. This module allows employees to record their daily working hours and overtime, if any. It provides transparency and accountability, ensuring accurate attendance and payroll processing.

**Key features include:**

Daily Time Entry: Employees can log in their working hours on a daily basis.

Approval Workflow: Time logs can be submitted for review and approved by reporting managers or team leads.

Integration with Attendance and Payroll: Logged hours are linked to attendance records and payroll calculations, ensuring accurate payment for regular and extra hours.

## Time Sheet Entry

Employees can enter their monthly working hours on a daily basis and submit for supervisor approval. It can be saved as draft also. If it’s submitted for approval and the supervisor approves it, then the entries made cannot be changed by the employee. During month end, all the entries have to be made and submit for full month approval. There is also provision to mark whether the employee is working from office or home on each day.

## Time Sheet Approval

This screen lists out all the direct reports and displays their time-sheets of the chosen month. Time sheets submitted by team members can be approved by clicking on the save button. If an employee needs to change the approved time sheet, supervisor can choose the particular employee and remove the approval. And then the employee can make changes. During month end, the supervisor has to ensure that full month time sheet has been submitted by team members and upload the time sheets for attendance processing. Work from home days are highlighted in the list. After uploading, the list will be cleared. The time sheets will be available in the employer portal after upload.

Supervisor will not be able to upload time sheet if any leave applications or off day swap request are pending for that particular month. Only one level approval is needed for time sheets.

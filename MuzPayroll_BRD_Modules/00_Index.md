# MUZPAYROLL - PRD

MUZPAYROLL package is specifically designed to manage end-to-end payroll processes efficiently while ensuring statutory compliance and data accuracy. The Payroll Module is a core component of any HRMS that automates and manages the complete salary process of employees. It ensures accurate computation of earnings, deductions, taxes, and net pay based on predefined salary structures and attendance data. Integrated with attendance, leave, and compliance modules, the payroll module streamlines monthly payroll processing, generates payslips, handles statutory contributions (such as PF, ESI, TDS, and Professional Tax), and facilitates direct bank transfers. It also supports gratuity & bonus calculations, arrears, reimbursements, and full-and-final settlements. By minimizing manual errors and ensuring timely payments, the payroll module enhances efficiency, maintains statutory compliance, and improves employee satisfaction.This package is ideal for organizations seeking to improve accuracy, compliance, and productivity while reducing manual HR tasks.

## Employee Portal

The Employee Self-Service Portal is a user-friendly, secure platform that empowers employees to access and manage their personal, professional, and payroll-related information independently. It reduces dependency on HR for routine tasks and enhances transparency and efficiency across the organization.

**Key Features:**

Personal Profile Management - View and update personal details like address, contact info, bank details, and emergency contacts.

Access statutory details (PAN, PF, ESI, etc.) and upload supporting documents.

Leave and Attendance - Apply for leave, view leave balances, and track approval status.

Compensatory Off - Apply for comp offs by entering the overtime hours, check status of comp off application etc.

Off Day Swap - Off days can swapped by submitting the application, check status etc.

Regularize attendance, view monthly attendance summaries, and check shift schedules.

Payroll & Salary - Download payslips, view salary structure, tax projections, and annual salary reports.

Submit investment declarations and access Form 16.

Loan/Advance Requests - Apply for salary advance or loan, view repayment schedules, and track approval status.

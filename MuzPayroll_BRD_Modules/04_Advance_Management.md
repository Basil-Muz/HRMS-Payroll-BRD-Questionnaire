# Advance Management

## Advance Management Module

The Advance Management Module is designed to manage and streamline the process of providing salary advances or employee loans. This module enables employees to request advances digitally, and helps HR and Finance teams track, approve, disburse, and recover such amounts efficiently.

**Key Features:**

Advance Request Portal: Employees can apply for salary advances or loans with just a few clicks.

Eligibility Check: System-based validations to check eligibility based on salary, tenure, and company policy.

Approval Workflow: Configurable multi-level approval process involving reporting managers, HR, and Finance.

Disbursement Tracking: Integration with payroll or accounting system for timely disbursement.

EMI & Recovery Scheduling: Automatic calculation of EMIs and integration with payroll for monthly deductions.

Loan Ledger: Detailed record of each employee’s outstanding advance amount, repayment history, and upcoming deductions.

Policy Configuration: Admin panel to define loan types, interest (if applicable), repayment period, and maximum eligibility.

**Benefits:**

Transparent and traceable advance request and repayment process.

Reduced manual intervention and errors.

Better financial planning for both employees and the organization.

Ensures compliance with company policy on employee financial assistance.

Recommendation Authority

# Compensatory Offs (Employee Portal)

## Compensatory Offs

The Compensatory Off (Comp-Off) Management Module in the employee portal facilitates the systematic tracking, approval, and utilization of compensatory leave earned by employees who work beyond regular working hours, especially on holidays or weekends.

**Key Features:**

Comp-Off Entry: Employees can request comp-off by logging extra hours worked, along with relevant dates and task details.

Manager Approval: Requests are routed to reporting managers for review and approval based on work validity and organizational policies.

Leave Balance Tracking: Once approved, the comp-off hours are converted into leave credits, which are added to the employee's comp-off balance.

Utilization & Expiry: Employees can apply for comp-off through the leave module. Comp offs can be encashed also.

Audit Trail: Maintains a transparent record of earned, approved, and availed comp-offs for internal audits and employee reference.

Integration with Attendance: Cross-checks with time logs or attendance data to ensure legitimate comp-off claims.

## Claim Compensatory Off

Through this screen, an employee can log extra hours worked and apply for a compensatory off. Comp off type needs to be chosen as there are different types of comp offs to meet project requirements. Date and start time, end time, break hours, work details also to be entered before submitting the request. If the overtime hours is more than 4 hours, then break time is mandatory. As per the organization policy, comp off claims have to be made within 7 days of the overtime work.

> **Open item flagged in BRD:** New requirement - A limitation of this screen is that start time and end time needs to be given in 24 hour time format which creates confusion among employees. This needs to be in 12 hour format.

Another issue with the current system is that, if a person starts over time at night and continues after 12 AM, it cannot be logged in as a single entry as the start date and end date are different. There needs to be a solution for this.

## Recommendations & Rejections

Comp off approval is a two level process similar to leave applications. Immediate supervisor has to recommend and reporting head needs to sanction the comp off claims. Through this screen, the first level supervisor recommends/rejects the claims made by direct reports after verifying the authenticity of claims. This process can happen on a daily basis as and when such claims arise.

## Comp Off Approval/Rejection

Approval of comp off claims by the reporting head can be done through this screen. All the comp off claims made by an employee in a particular month is consolidated and displayed here. From date is the date of first claim in a month and to date is the date of last claim. Claims are consolidated month wise only. Total overtime hours of a month is displayed and the quantum of comp off allocated is also calculated and shown.

4 hours of overtime qualifies for half a comp off

7.5 hours qualifies for a full day comp off

A provision to view all the claims in a month is available on clicking on the comp off days, which helps reporting head to verify the authenticity. Claims can be approved or rejected at this stage. After approval, the entry is listed in the employer portal comp off entry screen, from where the HR credits it to the employee’s leave count.

Comp off approvals of a month are done only after 7th of the following month as it is allowed to enter the comp offs before the expiry of seven days. If the claims are approved before 7th, the continuity of consolidation will be lost and there is a probability of employee losing the overtime hours.

## Claim Comp Off Register

This is a report available in the employee portal to view the status of all comp off claims. Reports can be generated for all, availed and unavailed comp offs.

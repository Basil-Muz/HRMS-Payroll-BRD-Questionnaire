## Payroll Process

Payroll process is the systematic procedure by which an organization calculates, manages, and disburses employee salaries. It involves multiple steps to ensure accurate and timely payment of wages while complying with applicable labor laws and statutory regulations.

**Key Steps in the Payroll Process:**

Employee Information Management

Collection and maintenance of employee details such as designation, salary structure, attendance, leaves, and tax declarations.

Attendance and Leave Calculation

Recording of days worked, leaves taken, holidays, and off days to determine payable days for the month.

Salary Computation

Calculation of gross salary, deductions (such as PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS), and net payable salary based on predefined salary structures.

Statutory Compliance

Ensuring compliance with labor laws and remitting statutory contributions like Provident Fund, ESI, and tax deductions within due dates.

Payslip Generation

Creating and distributing payslips to employees with a detailed breakup of earnings and deductions.

Payment Processing

Disbursal of salaries through bank transfers or other approved payment methods.

Reporting and Record Keeping

Maintaining payroll records, wage registers, and generating reports for audits, finance, and statutory filings.

## Salary Structure & Process

Muziris follows the cost to company (CTC) concept. CTC consist of gross salary plus employer contributions to PF and ESI. Payroll period is from first of the month to last day of the month. Certain companies process payroll from 25th of a month to next month 25th to ensure that salary is credited by end of the month to employee accounts.

Gross Salary & Allowances - Gross salary consist of different allowances including Basic, DA, HRA, Travelling Allowance, Bonus etc. In Muziris, Baisc + DA is a single component. Allowances can be added or removed from the organization salary structure time to time depending upon organization policy change or changes that happen in labour laws and income tax rules.

Currently all the allowances are fixed amounts. Allowances like DA can be variable according to the price index published by government in regular intervals. Many companies have variable DA.

All allowances except position allowance and washing allowance are given to all employees. Washing allowance is given to ESI covered employees only and Position allowance is given to ESI exempted employees only.

Baisc, DA and CCA are mandatory components in the salary structure and form part of minimum wages.

Bonus is an allowance that is included to cover the bonus liability.

HRA and LTA has TDS implications as employees can claim exemption on these allowances.

Need a new option to generate salary split ups by giving formulas to define the allowances. For example - Basic shall be 40% of Gross Salary or HRA shall be 50% of Basic.

Deductions - Deductions can be statutory or general. Statutory deductions are employee contributions to PF and ESI, profession tax and TDS. Any statutory deductions that may come in the future also.

LOP - Loss of Pay

Loss of Pay (LOP) refers to a situation where an employee’s salary is deducted for days of absence that are not covered under approved leave entitlements. Loss of pay is calculated based on the number of unpaid leave days or unauthorized absences in a month. LOP is effected in each allowance. And the total of each allowance forms the gross salary.

LOP Calculation formula - (Monthly Allowance Amount/No. Of working days or Month Days) x Number of LOP days

Employee PF Contribution - Statutory Employee contribution to PF is 12% of PF Gross limited to a maximum contribution of Rs. 1,800/-. PF Gross is a component derived by Muziris to ensure that PF contribution is not limited to 12% of Basic + DA which can attract PF legal proceedings in the future. This is calculated by adding other allowances to Basic + DA to make the amount equal to Rs. 15,000/-. (This is done because PF ceiling for statutory contribution is Rs. 15,000/- and if Basic + DA is less than 15,000 and there are other allowances that makes gross salary above 15,000/-, contribution needs to be paid on minimum 15,000/- statutorily.) HRA and Bonus can be exempted from being added to calculate the PF gross as these two are excluded from the purview of PF provided these allowances shall not be greater than 20% of Basic plus DA.

Apart from statutory contribution, employees can make voluntary pf contribution at a maximum of 100% of Basic plus DA.

Employee ESI Contribution - Employee ESI contribution is calculated at 0.75% of Gross salary. But washing allowance can be exempted from Gross Salary. In order to be eligible for ESI coverage, Gross salary shall be less than or equal to 21,000/-. ESI has two contribution periods. April to September and October to March. If the gross salary increases and crosses 21,000/- due to an increment in between a contribution period, ESI coverage needs to be continued till the end of the ongoing contribution period and contribution needs to be paid on the new gross salary - washing allowance.

Profession Tax - Profession Tax is a state-level tax levied on individuals earning income through employment, profession, trade, or business. It is applicable in many Indian states and is governed by the respective state laws. For salaried employees, the employer is responsible for deducting the profession tax from salaries and remitting it to the state government. Profession tax needs to be paid on half yearly salary. Employer has to make the payment to the government for April to September on or before August 31st and for October to March on or before February 28th. When doing the payment actual salary for first months are considered and 6th month salary needs to be estimated. In Muziris, deduction from employee salary is made on August and February. Certain companies follow monthly deduction. Gross Salary is considered for the calculation of profession tax.

When an employee is relieved except on March and September, half yearly profession tax till that month needs to be deducted from salary.

## Bonus Process

In Kerala, bonus payments to employees working in shops and commercial establishments are governed primarily by the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, along with applicable provisions under the Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1960.

**Applicability:**

The Payment of Bonus Act applies to all establishments with 10 or more employees.

Employees drawing a monthly salary (Basic + DA) or wage up to ₹21,000 are eligible for bonus.

The employee must have worked for at least 30 days in the accounting year to qualify.

**Key Provisions:**

**Minimum and Maximum Bonus:**

Minimum: 8.33% of annual wages or salary.

Maximum: 20%, depending on the employer's profits.

If the company is making loss, it will be exempted from making bonus payment for the first five years of its inception. After that period, even if loss making, bonus payment is mandatory.

**Calculation Basis:**

Bonus is calculated based on the salary or wage, capped at ₹7,000/month or the minimum wage, whichever is higher.

**Time of Payment:**

Bonus must be paid within 8 months from the close of the financial year.

**Set-off and Set-on:**

If the employer makes higher profits, they can pay more bonus (up to 20%) and carry forward any excess or shortfall to adjust over the next 4 years. This rule needs to be elaborated after discussion with the client.

## Gratuity Process

Gratuity is a statutory benefit provided to employees as a token of appreciation for their long-term service. It is governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 in India.

**Applicability:**

Applies to establishments with 10 or more employees.

Employees are eligible if they have completed a minimum of 5 years of continuous service with the same employer.

Gratuity is payable on retirement, resignation, death, disablement, or termination (except in cases of misconduct).

**Eligibility Criteria:**

The employee must be in continuous service for at least 5 years (not required in case of death or disablement).

Applies to both salaried and wage-earning employees.

**Calculation of Gratuity:**

Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Salary × Years of Service) / 26

Last Drawn Salary includes Basic + Dearness Allowance.

Number of years is rounded to the nearest full year (6+ months is considered a full year).

The salary is divided by 26 (working days in a month) to get the daily wage.

**Maximum Limit:**

The maximum amount of gratuity payable under the Act is ₹20 lakhs.

**Taxation:**

Gratuity received under the Act is tax-free up to ₹20 lakhs for employees covered under the Act.

Employer’s Responsibility:

Must pay gratuity within 30 days of it becoming due.

Failure to pay on time attracts interest and penalties.

When relieving date is marked for an employee, his/her gratuity shall be processed if eligible for payment. Currently there is provision available to process gratuity under the following conditions:

On exit

As on date to find out the gratuity liability of organization

On superannuation

On a specific date.

These provisions are to be retained.

## Salary Arrear Process

In Muziris, salary increments are generally done annually. The increment due month is available in the employee screen. If in any case, the increment is delayed due to issues other than performance issues, company decides to pay salary arrears. This is purely the discretion of the company. Salary arrears is calculated by multiplying the amount of increment by number of months delayed.

## Incentives Process

Muziris declares performance/project incentives or retention bonus to certain employees as part of its employee rewards and recognition program. These incentives are generally a fixed amount every month for a specific period of time. Incentives need to be processed every month, but this process is detached from payroll process as the incentives payment is credited separately on a different date. But these incentives need to appear in the employee pay slip as project incentives, not as part of gross salary though. But definitely included in CTC.

Section 1: Salary Head
1
MISSING WORKFLOW Salary Head — Who Can Create and Edit an Ordinary (Non-System-Defined) Component?
The stakeholder has already confirmed why System-Defined Salary Head components (e.g. Basic Salary) are locked — they're tied to statutory minimum-wage compliance and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone through the normal UI. What remains open is who has permission to create, edit, or delete an ordinary component that isn't flagged System Defined.
Confirmed (free text) MR-046
Both Super Admin and HR Admin can create a new, ordinary (non-System-Defined) Salary Head component. Once created, no role can edit or delete it through the normal UI — the only available change is toggling it between Active and Inactive.
